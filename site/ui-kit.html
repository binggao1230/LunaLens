Type one family

Geist for all text, Geist Mono for numbers, coordinates and timestamps. There is no serif face. Labels are small, uppercase and wide-tracked. The wordmark is LUNA · mark · LENS in Geist light.

Colours tokens

Blue is the only interactive colour: buttons, selected rows, active toggles, focus rings. Amber, blue and green also appear as data colours, meaning Sun, Earth and link; they never mark a button.

Buttons pills

Primary is the one blue action per screen (Validate, Send probe, Next). Ghost is every other button. Small ghost buttons use .btn-xs. Both are pills; inputs and cards are not.

Cards and rows surfaces

Cards: --surface on --page, 1 px --border, 12 px corners. Selected state: blue border or blue left bar plus --accent-soft fill. Education mode drops the boxes and keeps hairline dividers.

Mascot guide

The guide uses the team's three traced poses (assets/mascot/). Talking is the default, cheering is celebrating, alert is worried; sleeping dims the talking pose.
