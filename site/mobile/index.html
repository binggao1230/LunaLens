02What do these results mean?＋

Sunlight

The Sun counts as visible when its upper edge clears the local terrain horizon.

Earth in view

This is a direct geometric view to Earth's centre. It does not check antennas or a complete radio link.

Together

The overlap shows hours when both conditions are true. It is a useful planning clue, not a landing or power guarantee.

Terrain horizons are regional estimates. Local rocks and landing-point details may differ.
