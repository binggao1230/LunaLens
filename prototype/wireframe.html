Luna Lens — wireframes (layout only)

Low-fidelity layout for the real product. Boxes show where things go and who builds them, not how they look; visual design belongs to the designer. Numbers match the table at the bottom and doc/WORKPLAN.md §2.

Frontend AFrontend B

View 1 — Explore (one scenario in detail). Desktop and phone.

1App bar — logo · tabs: Explore | Compare | Date slip | Data & accuracy
2Scenario tray — chips: [A Shackleton ridge · 1 Jan] [B Mons Mouton · 1 Jan] [+ add]. Tap a chip to make it active.
3Map — toggle: South pole | Whole Moon. Site markers, tap to pick a point, lighting follows time, arrows to Sun and Earth.
4Site picker — curated list in groups (Apollo, CLPS, other nations, Artemis) + custom lat / lon fields.
5Scenario settings — landing date & time (UTC), mast height, link margin.
6Status tiles — Sun elevation · disk visible · sunset in · Earth elevation · link OK / blocked · Earthset in.
7Horizon panorama — compass direction × elevation. Terrain silhouette, no-terrain line, Sun and Earth now + tracks.
8Stay timeline — sunlight strip + Earth-link strip over the lunar day, with stay totals (sunlit %, longest dark, link %).
9Time scrubber (sticky, shared by all views) — ▶ play · slider · jump: next sunset / sunrise / Earthset / Earthrise · current UTC time.

View 2 — Compare (2–4 scenarios side by side)

1App bar + 2 scenario tray
10Comparison table — one row per scenario on a shared time axis: sunlight strip, link strip, then sunlit %, longest sunlit, longest dark, link %, longest link gap. Best value per column marked. Tap a row to open it in Explore.
11Scenario actions — duplicate with another date · duplicate with another site · remove.
9Time scrubber (moves a cursor through every row)

View 3 — Date slip (what if the landing moves?)

1App bar + 2 scenario tray
12Thresholds — "need at least N days of continuous sunlight" · "need link at touchdown: yes / no" · slip range.
13Slip chart — one bar per day of delay: sunlight available from touchdown. Days that meet the thresholds are marked as viable windows. Second small chart: link share.
14Use this day — tap a bar → "add as scenario" sends that date to the tray and to Compare.

View 4 — Data & accuracy

15Trust panel — data sources, DEM resolution and limits, our angles vs. JPL Horizons, our sunlit share vs. the PGDA illumination map, glossary.
